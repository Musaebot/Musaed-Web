# Pricing — Musaed (مساعد)

All nine systems are free for every Discord server, with no time limit and no card
required. Pro is an optional tier that raises some per-server limits and adds a custom
embed style. The landing page's #plans panel shows Pro as "coming soon" with no numeric
price; the limits below come straight from the bot's plans table and are more detailed
than what the panel itself shows.

Last updated: 2026-09-24

## Free

- Price: 0
- Included: all nine systems (moderation, automatic protection, account-age gate, welcome, support tickets, captcha verification, auto-responses, text shortcuts, trap channel) at full access
- Text shortcuts: up to 7
- Ticket types: up to 6
- Ticket panels: 1
- Quick-open buttons per panel: not available
- Auto-responses: up to 20
- Auto-response reply length: up to 15 words
- Embed style: the bot's default color

## Pro

- Price: not yet announced. Assigned by the Musaed team directly; there is no self-serve
  checkout or purchase flow yet.
- Text shortcuts: up to 14
- Ticket panels: up to 3
- Quick-open buttons per panel: up to 3
- Auto-responses: up to 40
- Auto-response reply length: up to 50 words
- Embed style: custom (brand color + footer)
- Ticket types: unchanged at 6

## Notes

- Early Access is not a Pro perk. It grants no higher limits, and its own features may change or become paid.
- Full limits and terms for each plan are governed by https://musaed.dev/terms.html and https://musaed.dev/privacy.html.
